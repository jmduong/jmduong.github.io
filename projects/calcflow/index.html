CalcFlow - a VR 3D Graphing Calculator

A VR 3D graphing calculator that lets users explore linear math concepts in an immersive environment, where equations are entered through an interactive calculator interface and instantly visualized as dynamic graphs on x, y, and z axes. It enables users to manipulate variables in real time, view geometric relationships in three dimensions, and learn core linear algebra and function behavior through spatial, hands-on interaction rather than traditional 2D graphs.
