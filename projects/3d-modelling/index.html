3D Modelling

I have been creating 3D models as a hobby, building objects, characters, and environments using tools like Blender. I enjoy turning ideas or reference images into 3D shapes, then refining them by adding detail, textures, and materials to make them look more polished and visually interesting.

I’ve experimented with different parts of the workflow, including modeling, basic UV unwrapping, and rendering. I’m especially interested in learning how to make models more optimized and better suited for games or real-time applications.

Most of my work is self-directed, focused on improving my skills and trying out new techniques through personal projects and experimentation.
